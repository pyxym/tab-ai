# Privacy Policy for TabQuest

**Last Updated:** October 14, 2025

## Overview

TabQuest ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our browser extension.

## Information Collection and Use

### What We Collect

TabQuest operates **entirely locally** on your device. We collect and store the following data **only on your local browser**:

1. **Tab Information**
   - Tab titles and URLs
   - Tab grouping and organization preferences
   - Favicon URLs for visual display

2. **User Preferences**
   - Custom categories you create
   - Category colors and names
   - Domain-to-category mappings
   - Language preferences (English, Korean, Japanese)

3. **Temporary Data**
   - Saved tab snapshots
   - AI-generated insights (computed locally)
   - Undo/redo history

### What We DO NOT Collect

- ❌ No personal identification information
- ❌ No browsing history sent to external servers
- ❌ No data transmitted to third parties
- ❌ No analytics or tracking data
- ❌ No cookies or tracking mechanisms
- ❌ No user accounts or authentication data

## Data Storage

All data is stored using Chrome's built-in storage APIs:

- **Chrome Sync Storage**: Used for user preferences and categories (synced across your Chrome installations if Chrome Sync is enabled)
- **Chrome Local Storage**: Used for temporary data like snapshots and insights

**Important:** No data is ever sent to external servers. All processing happens locally in your browser.

## Data Sharing

We do **NOT** share, sell, rent, or trade your data with any third parties. Period.

## Required Permissions

TabQuest requires the following Chrome permissions to function:

1. **tabs** - To access and organize your browser tabs
2. **storage** - To save your preferences and categories locally
3. **activeTab** - To interact with the currently active tab
4. **tabGroups** - To create and manage Chrome tab groups
5. **windows** - To access tabs across different browser windows
6. **sidePanel** - To provide the persistent side panel interface

These permissions are used **exclusively** for the extension's core functionality and never for data collection or tracking.

## Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

- Data security depends on your device's security
- We recommend keeping your browser and operating system up to date
- No data transmission means no interception risk

## User Control

You have complete control over your data:

- **View Data**: All data is stored in Chrome's storage and can be inspected using Chrome DevTools
- **Delete Data**: Use the "Clear Data" option in extension settings to remove all stored data
- **Uninstall**: Uninstalling the extension removes all stored data

## Children's Privacy

TabQuest does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The extension is designed for general use and does not require age verification.
